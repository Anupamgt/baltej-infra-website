About

Turnkey contracting from design to handover

M/s Baltej Infra LLP is a turnkey contractor. One contract covers design, procurement, construction, and handover.

Offices in Jaipur and Panchkula. Site teams work across six states and one union territory, including mountain terrain in Himachal Pradesh.

Public sector clients
NHAI, SJVN, NHLML, AAI, East Central Railway
EPC contractors
KEC International, L&T PT&D, Sterlite Power, Techno Electric, G R Infraprojects, MVD Cementation
Corridors worked on
NH-3, NH-4, NH-5, NH-21A, NH-44, NH-88, NH-130, NH-154
Power sites worked on
Nathpa Jhakri hydro station and Sunni Dam project, both SJVN

Services

What we deliver

Each service runs as a standalone contract or as a package under a larger EPC contractor.

01

Highway construction

Carriageway works on national and state highways: earthwork, granular sub-base, bituminous layers, and pavement quality concrete. Also crash barriers, fencing, drainage, black-spot rectification, and restoration after floods and landslides.

Used by
NHAI, NHLML, G R Infraprojects, MVD Cementation
Standards
MoRTH specifications, IRC codes
02

Power civil works

Civil packages for hydro-power stations and transmission networks. Scope covers access roads, transformer and reactor foundations, firewalls, control buildings, and slope protection.

Used by
SJVN, KEC International, L&T PT&D, Sterlite Power, Techno Electric
Sites
Nathpa Jhakri hydro station, Sunni Dam project
03

Structural and geotechnical engineering

In-house design and detailing for retaining walls, slope protection, culverts, and highway structures. We review soil data and size foundations for plant and substation works.

Used for
Mountain roads, bridge approaches, plant foundations
Standards
IS codes, IRC codes
04

Civil works subcontracting

Package execution under larger EPC contractors. We supply crews, plant, and quality records, and we bill in the client measurement format.

Used by
G R Infraprojects, MVD Cementation, Gurbachan Singh Constructions, Mohan Mutha Infrastructures
Includes
Measurement books, RA bills, quality documentation
05

MEP estimation and coordination

Estimation and drawing coordination for mechanical, electrical, and plumbing scope. Covers quantity take-off, vendor comparison, and interface checks against civil work.

Used for
Buildings, plant and substation packages
Stage
Tender estimation through construction
